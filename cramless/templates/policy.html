Content & Privacy Policy

Who owns uploaded lectures

Lecture files you upload stay the property of the institution and instructors who created them. Cramless claims no ownership or licence over any uploaded content.

What we do with your uploads

Files are stored only to generate your personal summaries and flashcards. They are never shared with other students, published, sold, or used for any purpose beyond your own study.

Your responsibility

Only upload material you are permitted to use for personal study. If an institution or instructor asks us to remove content, we will.

Your account data

Your email, courses, study logs and scores are private to you. We do not share them with third parties.

Deleting content

Deleting a course removes its lectures, summaries and flashcards. Contact the team to delete your whole account.
